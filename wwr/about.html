About White Water Rafting

White water rafting is an exciting outdoor activity that involves navigating through fast-flowing rivers and rapids using an inflatable raft. It is a popular adventure sport that attracts thrill-seekers and nature enthusiasts alike. Participants work together as a team to paddle through the rapids, facing challenges and obstacles along the way.

History

White water rafting is an adventure sport that involves traveling down rivers in an inflatable raft. It began developing as a recreational activity in the early 20th century. It became more popular in the United states during the 1940s and 1950s. Today, it is enjoyed worldwide for recreation, tourism, and competition.
